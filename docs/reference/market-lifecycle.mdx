title: "Market lifecycle"
description: "How markets move through enabled, disabled, and settled states, and what those states mean for competitors."
---

Treat market state as part of your trading logic, not just display metadata.

Refresh via [GET /api/v1/markets](/rest/markets) and listen for `admin_message` pushes whenever ops changes a market or the event state.

## Market states

Each market returned by `/api/v1/markets` includes a `status` field.

### `enabled`

- the market is live
- new orders can be submitted
- existing resting orders can match
- the market appears in leaderboard and portfolio marking

### `disabled`

- the market is visible, but new orders are rejected
- existing state remains queryable
- competitors should stop sending new orders until the market is enabled again

### `settled`

- trading in that market is over
- operators enter a final per-share settlement value
- open positions are flattened against that settlement price
- resting open orders in that market are canceled as part of settlement
- PnL becomes realized for that market
- competitors should re-fetch portfolio, positions, fills, and leaderboard after settlement

## Competition-wide trading stop

Operators can also halt submissions across the entire exchange.

From your perspective, order rejects may arrive even though a market is still listed. Surface the reject message and don’t assume market data alone determines whether you can trade.

## Fields that matter before you trade

From `/api/v1/markets`, pay attention to:

- `market_id`
- `status`
- `tick_size`
- `min_order_quantity`
- `reference_price`
- `settlement_price`

`tick_size` and `min_order_quantity` directly affect whether order entry is accepted.

## Recommended client behavior

1. Fetch `/api/v1/markets` during startup.
2. Do not send orders for markets with `status = "disabled"` or `status = "settled"`.
3. Refresh market state after a reconnect or `resync_required`.
4. After settlement, re-bootstrap account state because positions and realized PnL may update instantly.

## Official standings

The live [GET /api/v1/leaderboard](/rest/leaderboard) reflects event-day standings.

Once ops settles the relevant markets, they may freeze the books and publish official final standings later. Treat settlement as the breakpoint where PnL for that market becomes stable.
