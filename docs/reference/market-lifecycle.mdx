---
title: "Market lifecycle"
description: "How markets move through enabled, disabled, and settled states, and what those states mean for competitors."
---

Competitors should treat market state as part of trading logic, not just display metadata.

You can always fetch the latest state from [GET /api/v1/markets](/rest/markets), and you may also receive an `admin_message` over WebSocket when an operator changes the market or competition state.

## Market states

Each market returned by `/api/v1/markets` includes a `status` field.

### `enabled`

- the market is live
- new orders can be submitted
- existing resting orders can match
- the market appears in leaderboard and portfolio marking

### `disabled`

- the market is visible, but new orders are rejected
- existing state remains queryable
- competitors should stop sending new orders until the market is enabled again

### `settled`

- trading in that market is over
- operators enter a final per-share settlement value
- open positions are flattened against that settlement price
- PnL becomes realized for that market
- competitors should re-fetch portfolio, positions, fills, and leaderboard after settlement

## Competition-wide trading stop

Operators can also stop new trading submissions across the whole exchange.

From the competitor side, that looks like order rejects even if a specific market is still listed. Your client should surface the reject message and avoid assuming that market data alone tells you whether order entry is currently allowed.

## Fields that matter before you trade

From `/api/v1/markets`, pay attention to:

- `market_id`
- `status`
- `tick_size`
- `min_order_quantity`
- `reference_price`
- `settlement_price`

`tick_size` and `min_order_quantity` directly affect whether order entry is accepted.

## Recommended client behavior

1. Fetch `/api/v1/markets` during startup.
2. Do not allow order entry on markets with `status = "disabled"` or `status = "settled"`.
3. Re-fetch market state after a reconnect or `resync_required`.
4. Refresh account state after settlement because positions and realized PnL may change immediately.
