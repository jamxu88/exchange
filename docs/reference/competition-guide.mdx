## Before trading

1. Grab your assigned API key from the operator.
2. Call [Get User](/rest/user) to confirm `username`, `trader_id`, and `role`.
3. Cache every market’s `market_id`, `status`, `tick_size`, `min_order_quantity`, and `reference_price` from [Get Markets](/rest/markets).
4. Bootstrap positions and limits with [Get Portfolio](/rest/portfolio).
5. Open a WebSocket if you need live book updates or user-specific events.

## While trading

### Rules that affect order entry

- `price`, `quantity`, and `remaining` are integer values.
- `side` is always `BUY` or `SELL`.
- Standard competitors have a fixed worst-case per-market net position limit of `+/-1000`.
- Prices must align to the market `tick_size`.
- Quantities must be at least the market `min_order_quantity`.
- Authenticated competitor operations are limited to `100 ops/sec` per API key. That budget is shared across REST account/trading calls and authenticated WebSocket trading messages.

### Recommended client split

Use REST for bootstrap and recovery:

- [Get User](/rest/user)
- [Get Portfolio](/rest/portfolio)
- [Get Open Orders](/rest/open-orders)
- [Get Fills](/rest/fills)
- [Get Markets](/rest/markets)

Use WebSocket for:

- aggregated price-level market data on the `l3`-named channel
- short batched `delta` updates, usually flushed about every `100ms`
- user `fill` events
- user `order_state` events
- low-latency order actions

Current limitation:

- one `l3` market subscription per connection
- the `l3` market-data feed is price-level only, not a public per-order L3 feed

### What a successful order means

When an order is accepted:

- it may fill immediately, rest on the book, or do both
- the REST response can include immediate `fills`
- `resting: true` means remaining quantity was posted to the book
- `order_state` and `fill` messages are the live account-state feed while your socket is healthy
- the market-data `snapshot` and `delta` feed is aggregated by price level, so use `order_state` for your own exact resting-order detail
- market-data `delta` messages can carry multiple ordered events, so validate `start_sequence` and then advance your local book sequence to `sequence`

## Recovery during the event

If you receive `resync_required`:

1. discard local book state for the affected market
2. resubscribe and replace local state from the next snapshot
3. refresh account state over REST if your user stream may have lagged

Use [Recovery And Resync](/reference/recovery-and-resync) as the exact recovery playbook.

## After settlement

When a market settles:

- expect trading to stop for that symbol
- open orders disappear
- positions flatten at the operator-entered `settlement_price`
- unrealized PnL for that market becomes realized

Refresh everything right away: [Get Markets](/rest/markets), [Get Portfolio](/rest/portfolio), [Get Positions](/rest/positions), [Get Open Orders](/rest/open-orders), [Get Fills](/rest/fills), and [Get Leaderboard](/rest/leaderboard).

Live `GET /api/v1/leaderboard` shows the event-day standings; ops may publish official final standings later once all relevant markets are settled and locked.
