## Before trading

1. Get your assigned API key from the operator.
2. Call [Get User](/rest/user) to verify `username`, `trader_id`, and `role`.
3. Call [Get Markets](/rest/markets) and cache `market_id`, `status`, `tick_size`, `min_order_quantity`, and `reference_price`.
4. Call [Get Portfolio](/rest/portfolio) to bootstrap positions and your position limit.
5. Open a WebSocket connection if you need live L3 updates or user-specific events.

## While trading

### Rules that affect order entry

- `price`, `quantity`, and `remaining` are integer values.
- `side` is always `BUY` or `SELL`.
- Standard competitors have a fixed worst-case per-market net position limit of `+/-1000`.
- Prices must align to the market `tick_size`.
- Quantities must be at least the market `min_order_quantity`.
- Authenticated REST account and trading routes are limited to `100 ops/sec` per API key.

### Recommended client split

Use REST for bootstrap and recovery:

- [Get User](/rest/user)
- [Get Portfolio](/rest/portfolio)
- [Get Open Orders](/rest/open-orders)
- [Get Fills](/rest/fills)
- [Get Markets](/rest/markets)

Use WebSocket for:

- L3 market data
- user `fill` events
- user `order_state` events
- low-latency order actions

Current limitation:

- one `l3` market subscription per connection

### What a successful order means

When an order is accepted:

- it may fill immediately, rest on the book, or do both
- the REST response can include immediate `fills`
- `resting: true` means remaining quantity was posted to the book
- `order_state` and `fill` messages are the live account-state feed while your socket is healthy

## Recovery during the event

If you receive `resync_required`:

1. discard local book state for the affected market
2. resubscribe and replace local state from the next snapshot
3. refresh account state over REST if your user stream may have lagged

Use [Recovery And Resync](/reference/recovery-and-resync) as the exact recovery playbook.

## After settlement

When a market settles:

- new trading stops for that market
- remaining open orders are canceled
- remaining open positions are flattened at the operator-entered `settlement_price`
- unrealized PnL for that market becomes realized

Immediately refresh:

- [Get Markets](/rest/markets)
- [Get Portfolio](/rest/portfolio)
- [Get Positions](/rest/positions)
- [Get Open Orders](/rest/open-orders)
- [Get Fills](/rest/fills)
- [Get Leaderboard](/rest/leaderboard)

Important distinction:

- [Get Leaderboard](/rest/leaderboard) is the live marked leaderboard during the event
- operators may publish official final standings separately after all relevant markets are settled and results are frozen
