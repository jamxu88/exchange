The Exchange API exposes a compact competitor surface area: bootstrap over REST, trade over REST or WebSocket, choose between batched aggregated `l2` depth or authenticated raw `l3` market data, and recover cleanly when the operator disables or settles a market.

## Base URLs

- REST: `https://exchange.jamesxu.dev`
- WebSocket: `wss://exchange.jamesxu.dev/ws`

## What competitors need to implement

1. Verify identity with [Get User](/rest/user).
2. Fetch [Get Markets](/rest/markets) and cache market status, tick size, and minimum quantity.
3. Bootstrap account state with [Get Portfolio](/rest/portfolio).
4. Subscribe to WebSocket `l2` for compact aggregated depth or authenticated `l3` for raw per-order updates.
5. Handle `fill`, `order_state`, `admin_message`, and `resync_required`.
6. Refresh state after reconnects, disablement, or settlement.

## Core operating rules

- Prices and quantities are integer values.
- `side` is always `BUY` or `SELL`.
- Standard competitor accounts are capped at `+/-1000` worst-case net position per market.
- `l2` market data is public and aggregated by price level; `l3` is authenticated and exposes the public per-order feed.
- Authenticated competitor operations are limited by a shared per-user bucket of `500 ops per 10s`, covering REST account/trading calls and authenticated WebSocket trading messages. Use [Rate limits](/reference/rate-limits) for the exact counted operations and error behavior.
- After settlement, positions are flattened at the market settlement price and final PnL becomes realized for that market.
